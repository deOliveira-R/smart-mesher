- **The real baseline for a native IR is missing: integrated project files that already persist all three layers.** Every row in the table is an exchange format or a library dump. Cubit's own `.cub5` (ACIS/OCC geometry + virtual topology + mesh + owner association + blocks/sidesets/BCs in one store), Salome's `.hdf` study (geometry + hypotheses + groups-on-geometry + mesh), Simmetrix `.smd/.sms`, and the open-source lineage CGM (Cubit's geometry layer with partition/composite virtual geometry) and ITAPS iGeom/iMesh/iRel + Lasso (relations between geometry and mesh as a separate typed interface, which is literally differentiator B) are absent; ITAPS is dismissed in one word. The positioning should read "like .cub5/iRel, but open, versioned, polyhedral-first", not "like CGNS".

- **Transport consumers on polyhedral/AMR meshes are under-covered, including the first consumer's actual conventions.** Missing: LLNL Teton/UMT (Sn upstream-corner-balance on unstructured zones; UMT takes Blueprint topologies through Conduit; verify whether Teton accepts arbitrary polyhedral zones and Blueprint HDF5), LANL Capsaicin/Jayenne and the Draco RTT mesh format (AMR polyhedral), Attila/Attila4MC (commercial tet Sn, a major Cubit nuclear customer), PROTEUS-SN (ANL, MOAB/MeshKit lineage) and MeshKit RGG (the existing reactor-lattice mesher), ORNL Shift. For Griffin: the MOOSE Reactor module (RGMB) encodes region_id/material_id/pin_id/assembly_id as Exodus extra element integers (a precedent for overlapping typed selections in L2), and CMFD needs a coarse mesh with fine-to-coarse element ids, i.e. a multi-level mesh hierarchy the layer model has no slot for.

- **CSG, universes/lattices and instancing are absent, although MC codes consume them natively.** OpenMC/MCNP/Serpent geometry is CSG with repeated structure (universes, lattices); L0 is B-rep only. CAD-to-CSG converters (GEOUNED, McCad, SuperMC/MCAM, cad-to-openmc) are not surveyed; a faceted DAGMC export discards exact quadrics. Unanswered: is the IR volume-mesh-first, surface-first (DAGMC), or does it also carry CSG and repetition (pin/assembly instancing for full cores, STEP assemblies)? This decides what L0/L1 store and whether L2 regions can be instanced.

- **High-order DG mesh formats that persist non-conformal pairings in the file contradict differentiator C as worded.** HOPR `.h5` (FLEXI/Fluxo/PICLas) stores mortar sides in SideInfo: nbElemID on the big side holds the mortar type (1/2/3) as a negative value, small master sides are listed in the owner's side block, slave SideType is negated, plus optional octree data (IsMortarMesh, ElemToTree, xiMinMax) (confirmed: hopr.readthedocs.io v1.2.1 §2.5). Also PyFR `.pyfrm` (interface arrays con_p0p1/bcon persisted), Nektar++ XML/HDF5 (.nekg) with curved edges/faces and composites, Nek5000 `.re2/.co2`, t8code (hybrid-element AMR forest, p4est successor), and for transfer weights ANSYS CFX GGI, DataTransferKit (VERA) and preCICE. The claim must become "no polyhedral-capable format persists pairings", not "only run-time or refinement-derived".

- **Formal mesh/topology abstractions the design borrows from are missing.** DUNE grid interface (Bastian et al. 2008): first-class intersections with inside/outside and conforming(), the canonical face->(left,right) across non-conforming meshes, plus dune-grid-glue (supermesh), dune-alugrid non-conforming refinement, dune-polygongrid, DGF format; DOLFINx topology (on-demand (d0,d1) connectivity, MeshTags, mixed-topology meshes) and Basix DOF transformations; Shards/Intrepid2 cell topologies and orientation tools (the existing baseline for "orientation as symmetry-group index" and L8 dof permutation); Lienhardt G-maps / CGAL Combinatorial Maps and Weiler's radial-edge structure for non-manifold L1. Only SGC and OVM half-faces are cited.

- **Silo, EnSight Gold and Tecplot are absent, and two of them store what differentiator A says no vis format stores.** Silo (LLNL): DBPutPHZonelist polyhedral zonelists with lo/hi ghost offsets, DBmaterial mixed cells with volume fractions, MRG trees (hierarchical named regions: a database-like set model and the ancestor of Conduit matsets/nestsets), multiblock, published manual. Tecplot FEPOLYHEDRON zones carry face left/right element neighbours and boundary connections; EnSight Gold has nfaced/nsided parts. Add a row for each and amend (A).

- **Polyhedral geometry rules are left unspecified.** Nothing states whether cells must be convex, how non-planar faces are decomposed (centroid fan, triangulation, PWLD sub-tets), or whether the decomposition is stored. Different consumers will compute different face areas/normals/centroids, which breaks the overlap-table reciprocity invariant, the |Omega.n| upwind graph, and Serpent's convexity requirement; a declared decomposition rule and per-face planarity tolerance belong in the schema and validator.

- **Mixed-material cells conflict with the "material regions partition volumes exactly" invariant.** Overlay workflows (OpenMC material_volumes), homogenised cross sections, and Silo/Conduit matsets all need per-cell material volume fractions and optionally sub-cell interfaces. Unanswered whether L2 forbids this, models it as a matset-like table, or pushes it to L8 fields; the table's matsets column hints at the gap without resolving it.

- **Several claims are overstated or rest on unverified reading.** (F) "none has a validator or conformance suite": cgnscheck exists; openPMD (standard + openPMD-validator + HDF5/ADIOS2/JSON backends) is the model for F and is not cited. "No tool infers non-conformity from topology": checkMesh unmatched faces, libMesh find_neighbors, DUNE conforming() do. (A) "no orientation rule": OpenFOAM's owner->neighbour normal, CGNS/MED signed faces and OVM half-faces are rules, so the claim is only "unspecified in VTKHDF/Conduit/Exodus/MOAB". The Exodus NFACED side-set limit comes from code-reading, yet the export matrix routes Griffin through Exodus NFACED while Griffin's polyhedral support is an open item. (G) Arrow/HDF5 zero-copy compatibility is asserted with no design.

- **"Fast large-mesh I/O" has no evidence anywhere.** No throughput, file-size or scaling data for any format; HDF5 vs ADIOS2 BP5 vs Zarr vs Arrow/Parquet as the physical backend is not compared; 64-bit id modes (Exodus EX_ALL_INT64_*, CGNS cgsize_t builds, vtkIdType) and compression (ZFP/Blosc, HDF5 filters) are not discussed, although they decide whether billion-cell meshes are even representable in the export targets.

- **Stable identity and partitioning are asserted, not designed.** How ids survive refine/coarsen/repartition (libMesh unique_id, MOAB GLOBAL_ID, Omega_h SFC-based globals, p4est/t8code Morton ids, Exodus id maps; dense ints vs UUIDs) is unspecified; hash-versioned derived tables need a canonical ordering that is never defined; partitioners (ParMETIS, Scotch, Zoltan2, KaHIP) and their file conventions (Nemesis .nem/.pex, Fluent §40, MED joints) are absent from the L9 discussion.

- **Missing table columns and practical dimensions.** Units/scale (cm in MCNP/OpenMC/Serpent vs m in OpenFOAM; CGNS DimensionalUnits/DataClass is the only precedent), 2D/RZ axisymmetric and 1D meshes (common in reactor calculations; the IR is silent), time series for depletion/transient sequences (CGNS BaseIterativeData, Exodus time steps, VTKHDF temporal), mixed-dimension/internal faces/baffles (shell blocks, zero-thickness plates), and the adaptation ecosystem's classification format libMeshb/.meshb (VerticesOnGeometricTriangles with (u,v), Ridges/Corners/Required entities; MMG, NASA refine, Feflo.a) plus the ESP/CAPS attribute model (capsGroup on CAD faces -> BCs) for L3.
