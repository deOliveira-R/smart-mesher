### W3b-RT-ANALYTIC. `sm-rt` (L3): ANALYTIC tracker on exact CSG quadrics, tori, lattices and nested universes (no mesh), as a leaf of the shared hierarchy

- **Binding rulings.** Decision 12 (ANALYTIC and HIERARCHY are W3b; `TrackerCaps::ANALYTIC`, `TrackerKind::Analytic = 4` already frozen). `docs/research/delta-tracking.md` 6.1/6.2 and `backlog.md` "Ray tracing and delta tracking (W3b)": one hierarchy, one segment iterator; location starts from the owning node, never from the root. Internal cells are exact; the validator and `rt.check` judge geometry. No claim of a speedup over DAGMC or OpenMC: work counters only.
- **Deps (task):** W3b-RT-HIER (owns the `Hierarchy` walker in sm-rt: level stack, Rect/Hex lattice index and DDA, `Union` priority, instance/cell prefix numbering, `SlotChange`, the `Leaf` trait below, mesh leaf adapter) lands first. All sm-rt tasks edit one crate and serialize: HIER -> ANALYTIC -> DELTA. The stage-0 ACRs below land first. If HIER is NOT a separate task, this task also implements the `Hierarchy` pieces and the lattice acceptance (items 8-11) moves under it unchanged.
- **Deps (crate):** existing sm-rt deps; `sm-geom-api` already present (`CsgCell`, `HalfSpace`, `Quadric`, `ImplicitTorus`, `SurfaceDef`, `Frame`, `real_roots`). No new dependency, no sm-geom (L1) dependency needed; no sm-model, sm-tess, sm-meshio.
- **Idea.** A `CsgLeaf` is an OpenMC/MCNP universe: ordered cells, each a union of `CsgCell` (intersection of signed half-spaces over `GeomStore` surfaces). Tracking is surface tracking: distance to the nearest *relevant* surface along the ray, then relocate with the crossed surface's sense forced. Lattices, nesting and placements are NOT re-implemented: `WorldSpec` (frozen, `Union`/`Lattice` of `ComponentId`) describes them and the HIER walker drives; the CSG leaf only answers three questions in its local frame: who contains this point, how far to the next event, who is on the other side. Distances use closest-approach forms, never `b^2-4ac` on shifted origins, so chords stay at 1e-12 far from the origin.
- **Scope (mandatory):**
  1. **Builder.** `pub struct AnalyticTrackerBuilder` implements `TrackerBuilder` for `BuildInput::Hier` (ACR-2) whose leaves are `LeafInput::Csg(CsgLeafInput)` (ACR-1), including the degenerate world of one leaf. `builder_version()`, `cache_key` (kind Analytic; hash of implicit definitions by VALUE, cell DNF in input order, world, boundaries, options; independent of `EntityId` handles and thread count), `build -> (Box<dyn Tracker>, BuildReport)`, `load` returns `RtError::Unsupported("analytic tracker has no cache image")` (`export_cache` is `None`). Mixed worlds (CSG leaves under a mesh lattice and the reverse) are accepted whenever HIER accepts the leaf kinds.
  2. **Surface kinds** (private `kernel` module). Classify each distinct surface once at build from `SurfaceDef` and `ImplicitDef`: `Plane`, `Sphere`, `Cylinder` (any axis, via `Frame`), `Cone` (one frame, both nappes, apex handled), `Torus` (ring, `ImplicitTorus`), `General` (any `Quadric`). Per-kind ray kernel `fn hits(&self, o: Point3, d: Vector3, t_min: f64, out: &mut SmallVec<[f64;4]>)`, ascending `t`, `d` unit. Sphere/cylinder/cone use the perpendicular-offset form `m = q - (q.d) d` (as the stub's `Shape::interval`); `General` and `Torus` take `ImplicitSurface::ray_hits` roots then two Newton steps on compensated (FMA, Graillat) Horner residuals; double roots are tangent and produce NO event (zero-length chord, no cell change). `outward_normal(p)` per kind from the gradient. Sense = sign of `f(p)` with `HalfSpace::sign`.
  3. **Cells.** `CsgCellDef { region: CsgRegion (union of CsgCell), region_name }`, ordered, FIRST CONTAINING CELL WINS (priority is the stub's rule and the `Union` rule). Build computes per cell the *relevant surface list*: surfaces of its own terms plus, unless `disjoint` is declared, surfaces of earlier cells. CSR `surface -> cells` (ascending id) for neighbour search.
  4. **`locate(p, dir, hint, scratch)`** (leaf-local, called by HIER with the local point): (a) concentric fast path, detected at build: a leaf whose cells are nested by `Sphere` or coaxial `Cylinder` shells (the pin cell, TRISO layers) locates by one binary search on `r^2` plus the bounding planes; (b) otherwise evaluate cells in priority order using only the surfaces they name, `f` cached per surface for the call; (c) a point with `|f| <= tol.linear` on some surface is `on_face`; the sense of that surface is taken from `sign(grad f . dir)` (moving into), `dir = None` uses `hashed_direction(p)`; exact zero gradient is a build-time or locate-time `RtError`, never a panic. Two surfaces simultaneously on-face (edge/corner) both use the direction rule; remaining ties go to the lowest cell id. Outside every cell of the root: `CellId::NONE`, or `CellId::COMPLEMENT` when `ComplementPolicy::Region`.
  5. **`step`** (leaf-local event): distance = min over the relevant surfaces of the current cell of the first root `t > 0` (roots with `t <= 0`, and for the surface in `entered_via` roots with `t <= tol.linear / |d.n|` whose crossing does not re-enter, are skipped; other roots of the SAME surface, e.g. the far side of a torus tube, are kept); candidates compared in the leaf, then against the lattice wall by HIER (wall wins an exact tie: `SlotChange` before a surface event is the stub's order). After the hit, neighbour search: cells in `surface_cells[s]` with `s` forced to the post-crossing sense; next other surfaces within tolerance use the direction rule; then a full-leaf scan (counted in `AnalyticStats::slow_locates`); then `RecoveryPolicy` nudge along `dir` by `nudge_distance` and one re-locate; failure is `Lost(RecoveryFailed)`, an uncovered point `Lost(NoCellContainsPoint)` with `path_of` naming leaf, cell and the nearest surface (actionable via `rt.check`). Events map as in the stub: surface with distinct regions both sides = `RegionBoundary`, same region = `CellFace`, `BoundarySpec` surfaces = `Boundary` (Reflect/Wrap applied in step through `face_normal`, bit-exact for planes).
  6. **Tracker surface.** `caps = ANALYTIC | HIERARCHY` (plus `PERIODIC` when decks are on planes); `FaceId == SurfaceId`; `surfaces()` front/back from the cell DNF (sense of the half-space in the cell, `NONE` for outside); `cell_region`; `cell_volume` = caller-supplied `CsgCellDef::volume` else `None` (never Monte Carlo); `face_normal(f, p)` exact per kind; `closest(p, region, ..)` exact for Plane, Sphere, Cylinder, Cone, Torus (`|hypot(x,y) - R, z| -> hypot - r` in the torus frame), `RtError::Unsupported("closest on general quadric")` for `General`; `bin_map` as the contract default; `trace_bundle` fixed-block parallel from the contract. `bounds`: from the lattice box or root `bounds`; unbounded root is build issue `csg.unbounded`.
  7. **Segment iterator.** `pub(crate) trait Leaf { fn locate(&self, p, dir, hint, ctx) -> LeafLoc; fn next_event(&self, cell, p, dir, entered_via, max, ctx) -> LeafEvent; fn cross(&self, cell, event, p, dir, ctx) -> LeafLoc; fn cell_count(&self) -> usize }` is HIER's trait (adopt its exact names if already merged). `CsgLeaf` implements it and ALSO exposes `fn node_info(&self) -> LeafNodeInfo { bounds: Option<Aabb>, region_set: Vec<RegionId> }` so DELTA can build majorant nodes without a second traversal. Global `CellId` = prefix sum over instances in ascending `InstanceId` (leaf cell count `n_c`, instance base from memoized subtree counts, O(depth) per query, no table of size instances).
  8. **Build checks** (`BuildReport::issues`, located by leaf, cell, surface; none fatal unless noted): `csg.unbounded`, `csg.degenerate_surface` (zero radius, torus with `major <= minor`: fatal), `csg.empty_cell` (a cell whose DNF is provably empty by plane/sphere sign conflicts), `csg.unused_surface`, `csg.unknown_surface` (fatal), and `check_csg(&CsgLeafInput, &GeomStore, n_samples, seed) -> Vec<BuildIssue>`: fixed-seed (splitmix64 from the leaf hash) stratified samples report `csg.overlap` (two cells contain the point with `disjoint` declared) and `csg.gap` (no cell contains a point of the leaf bounds) with the sample point. Extend `RtPlugin` `rt.check` and `rt.info` to the analytic tracker (read-only; counters, caps, surfaces by kind).
  9. `crates/sm-rt/DESIGN.md` section "Analytic leaf": surface-kind table with kernel formulas and their conditioning, tolerance rules, tie rules, the three questions of `Leaf`, differences from OpenMC/MCNP (first-wins priority, no per-surface sense array, exact closest), and the list in "Deferred".
- **Acceptance (mandatory; analytic; absolute tolerance 1e-12 unless stated; fixtures built locally in `tests/common/csg.rs` with `sm_geom_api::MemoryStore`, seeds from the case name; origins within 50 units of the body):**
  1. **Sphere.** R = 5, centre off-origin; lines with impact parameter b in {0, 3, 4.9}, 1e5 seeded IUR lines: chord `2 sqrt(R^2 - b^2)`; Cauchy: mean chord over 1e6 IUR lines `= 4R/3` within 4 sigma.
  2. **Cylinder.** Finite cylinder (infinite cylinder cut by two planes), axis tilted by a seeded rotation: chord of a line in the cross-section plane `2 sqrt(R^2 - b^2)`; of a line through the axis at angle alpha to it `2R / sin(alpha)` (clipped by the planes: analytic min/max); coaxial annulus: sum of the two annular chords plus the bore equals the outer chord.
  3. **Cone.** Half-angle 30 deg, apex at p0, both nappes cut by planes: axis-parallel line at radial offset rho: inside-chord `z1 - max(z0, rho / tan(30 deg))` per nappe; a line through the apex hits it once (no spurious zero-length cell flip).
  4. **Torus** R = 3, r = 1, any placement (seeded rotation+translation, results rotation-invariant): axis-parallel line at rho in (R-r, R+r): `2 sqrt(r^2 - (rho-R)^2)`; equatorial lines with offset b < R-r: two chords of length `sqrt((R+r)^2 - b^2) - sqrt((R-r)^2 - b^2)`; R-r < b < R+r: one chord `2 sqrt((R+r)^2 - b^2)`; Cauchy: mean chord over 1e6 IUR lines through the (non-convex) torus `= 4V/S = 2r` within 4 sigma, with each chord counted separately. Tangent line at b = R + r: no event, state stays outside; at b = R + r - 1e-6 the chord matches the analytic value within 1e-9 (documented sqrt conditioning), never Lost.
  5. **General quadric** (ellipsoid with a seeded rotation, via `Quadric::from_local`): chord against the independent closed form from the semi-axes within 1e-12; hyperboloid of one sheet cut by two planes: analytic chords.
  6. **Point location exact.** 1e6 seeded points against an independent analytic predicate per primitive and for a 5-shell nested sphere + torus + cone leaf: identical cell unless within 1e-12 of a surface, where `on_face` is set; points placed ON a surface (constructed exactly, sphere at (R,0,0)) with `dir` in/out select the inside/outside cell; edge points (two surfaces) are deterministic and identical across repeated calls and thread counts; `locate(dir = None)` equals `hashed_direction` rule. The concentric fast path agrees with the generic path on 1e6 points, and `AnalyticStats::surface_evals` per locate on a 12-ring pin cell is `<= ceil(log2 12) + 3` (work counter, not time).
  7. **Union and first-wins.** An OpenMC-style cell `(sphere A) | (sphere B)` and a first-wins overlap pair give the analytic union chords (inclusion-exclusion on the line) within 1e-12; `check_csg` reports `csg.overlap` for a pair declared `disjoint` with overlap and `csg.gap` for a hole, each with the sample point inside the true overlap or gap.
  8. **Rect lattice, nested universes.** 17 x 17 pin lattice (pitch 1.26, fuel R 0.4, clad R 0.47, moderator elsewhere), then three levels (pin -> assembly 17x17 -> core 3x3): row line through pin centres: 17 fuel chords of 0.8, `SlotChange` count 16 per assembly row, total path length equals the box chord within 1e-12; `levels` hold (component, instance, lattice index) at every step, depth 3; global `CellId` equals the prefix-sum formula and `cell_count()` equals `sum instances x leaf cells`; `Lattice::locate_rect` and the tracker agree on 1e5 points including lattice walls (ties to the higher index); `outer` fill used outside the array.
  9. **Hex lattice** (`LatticeKind::Hex`, pointy-top axial `(q, r)`, flat-to-flat 1.4, pin in each hex, 3 rings in a parallelogram dims 5x5 with `outer` void in unused corners): chord lengths of lines through hex centres along the three lattice directions equal `n x` the pin diameter; a line through a hex vertex (three hexes meet) advances to the neighbour chosen by the rule "largest `d . n` among the tied planes, then lowest neighbour index" (documented, tested both ways), never Lost; for a flat-top lattice (ACR-4) the same tests hold with a rotated fixture; z-stack `dims[2] > 1` works.
  10. **Comparison against the stub.** For `sm_rt_api::stub::AnalyticWorld` worlds (sphere sets, box sets as 6-plane cells with first-wins background, flat, 3x2x1 and 4x4x4 `RectLattice`, reflective and vacuum decks): 1e5 seeded rays each through `trace`: same number of segments, equal `cell`, `region`, `StepEnd` kind and faces, `dist` within 1e-12; `locate` equal on 1e5 points; `cell_volume` equal where the stub gives `Some`. The CSG numbering of local cells is shapes then background so global ids are equal without mapping.
  11. **Sums and shared faces.** 1e6 seeded lines through a 3-level core: sum of segment lengths per line equals the analytic hull chord within 1e-12, 0 `Lost`, 0 `Err`; reversed line gives identical chords (reversed order) within 1e-12; lines within 1e-9 of a surface edge or a lattice wall corner never end `Lost`.
  12. **Boundaries and decks.** Reflective box around a pin: 1e4 histories of 1e3 steps stay inside and lose none; translation `Wrap` pair on planes wraps within 1e-12; `Respond` boundary is not applied in step and `resolve_boundary(White)` returns a unit vector inward.
  13. **Errors, determinism, allocation.** Every error and build-issue code used here has a test; `sm_testkit::determinism::check` on the `trace_bundle` `content_hash()` of a fixed 1e5-ray batch, `RAYON_NUM_THREADS=1` vs default and repeated, and on `structure_hash`; `structure_hash` unchanged when surface handle ids are permuted; counting-allocator test: `step`/`locate`/`closest` with a warmed `Scratch` perform 0 allocations at depth <= `LEVEL_INLINE`.
  14. **Closest.** Distances to plane, sphere, cylinder, cone, torus equal the closed forms within 1e-12 on 1e4 random points; the returned `point` lies on the surface (`|f| <= 1e-12 * scale`); general quadric returns `Unsupported`.
  15. **Record-only (no gate), release, 1 and 4 threads, CPU model recorded in DESIGN.md:** segments/s and surface-evals per segment for the 17x17 pin lattice and a 3-level core; `locate` points/s with and without the concentric fast path; `slow_locates` per 1e6 segments (expected 0 on clean geometry); torus chords/s. These feed the honest DAGMC/OpenMC benchmark task, they do not claim anything here.
- **Stretch (after mandatory 1-14):** (S1) `AnalyticStats` exposed through `rt.info`; (S2) `closest` lower bound for general quadrics via `|f| / sup |grad f|` flagged `exact: false`; (S3) per-leaf AABB prefilter to skip surface tests for far cells (counter-visible).
- **Must NOT:** edit any contract crate (`sm-rt-api`, `sm-geom-api`, `sm-model-api`, `sm-testkit`); duplicate `Lattice`, `WorldSpec`, `Level` or `Placement` types; re-implement the lattice DDA or level stack (HIER owns them); use wall-clock gates; use `HashMap` iteration order; use `unsafe`; `unwrap` on input paths; clamp a failed root polish to hide an error; start `locate` at the root during a step; claim a speedup over DAGMC/OpenMC.
- **Deferred (state in DESIGN.md):** delta tracking and majorants (W3b-RT-DELTA); NURBS and general implicit surfaces other than `Quadric`/torus; MCNP/OpenMC CSG import (sm-cadio) and export (an exporter node); volumes by exact integration; `export_cache` for analytic worlds; tori with `major <= minor` (spindle/horn); SIMD/packet traversal; model-instancing -> `WorldSpec` bridge (`sm-model-api` `Layout::Hex` rings with sparse slots maps to a covering parallelogram plus `outer`; owned by the integration step); `Boundary` wrap on curved surfaces.
- **Registers:** `AnalyticTrackerBuilder`, `check_csg`, `AnalyticStats`; `RtPlugin` `rt.check` and `rt.info` extended to analytic trackers. No new op, no new MCP tool (the existing `rt.*` read-only queries cover it).
