### W3b-RT-HIER. `sm-rt` (L3, existing crate; additive modules): HIERARCHY backend with delta tracking, majorant hierarchy, hybrid policy, region jump, safety distance, World/lattice instancing

- **Binding rulings.** Decision 12 (HIERARCHY is W3b; the `.smrt` sidecar stays the cache; no protocol `tracking` field). User direction: internal cells exact; polyhedral output for an in-house Sn/MOC + Monte Carlo code; ray tracing that beats DAGMC, benchmarked honestly (this task builds the structure and records numbers; it claims no speedup). Backlog "Ray tracing and delta tracking". `docs/research/delta-tracking.md` §6 is the design source; `tracking-landscape.md` §3 claims 4-6 and 9 are the claims this task makes measurable.
- **Names come from `sm-rt-api` after the stage-0 batch below.** If the frozen spelling differs, the frozen spelling wins; a missing capability is `status: blocked_on_api` with an `api_change_request`. Never duplicate a contract type. Physics data never enters `sm-rt`: cross sections arrive only through the `CrossSections` trait.
- **Deps.** No new crates. sm-rt keeps sm-core, sm-math, sm-geom-api, sm-topo-api, sm-mesh-api, sm-model-api, sm-rt-api, sm-op-api, sm-layer-api, rayon, smallvec, serde, schemars, serde_json. Dev: sm-testkit (`reference::hex_block`, `meshes::mixed_transition`, `surf3::SplitMix64`, `determinism::{check, par_map, hash_f64_bits}`), `sm-rt-api` with `stubs` (`AnalyticWorld::with_lattice` as the exact oracle, `stub::TableXs`), proptest via `sm_testkit::strategies::config(n)`.
- **Idea.** One segment iterator over a disjoint hierarchy, two segment modes. A *surface* segment is one cell with `sigma_maj = sigma_t(cell)`, so nothing is ever rejected and the track-length estimator is valid (this is today's walk). A *delta* segment is one leaf box of a kd partition (or one lattice element) with `sigma_maj = M[mset][band]`, a bound over every material below it. A flight is the sampling loop on this stream. Mode is a pure function of (node, band, current material), never of history, thread or time. Location at a tentative collision starts at the node that owns the majorant and never at a root. Existing `step`/`locate`/`trace` output for inputs without hierarchy options is unchanged bit for bit.
- **Scope (mandatory, deliver in this order; M0-M3 gate acceptance 1-12, M4 gates 13-15; if M4 slips report `status: partial` with M0-M3 complete):**
  1. **M0 surface iterator.** `MeshTracker::segments` implements `DeltaTracker::segments` in `TrackingMode::Surface` as a wrapper over `step`: each yielded `MajorantSegment{t0, t1, sigma_maj, mode: Surface, node, cell, region, mset}` equals the `trace` `Segment` (cell, region, t0, length) bit for bit; `sigma_maj` is `xs.total(material(cell), q)` evaluated once per segment.
  2. **M1 material sets, majorant tables, kd partition** (`hier/`: `mset.rs`, `table.rs`, `kd.rs`).
     - `MaterialMap` (PerRegion or PerCell, validated against `cell_count`/`regions().0.len()`; bad length or unknown id is `RtError::Build` with code `hier.material_map`). Material sets are sorted-deduplicated `Vec<MaterialId>` in a `BTreeMap`, id by first appearance in ascending node order (never `HashMap`).
     - Tables `M[mset][band]` as f32 rounded up (`next_up` after the f64 to f32 cast; non-finite or negative `band_max` is `RtError::Build` `xs.nonfinite` naming material and band). Invariant: `f64::from(M) >= band_max(m, b)` for every m in the set and `M <= band_max * (1 + 2^-23)` for the maximizing m.
     - kd partition over the model box padded by `tol.linear`: node boxes are disjoint and cover the box. A leaf stores its sorted candidate cell list (CSR; a cell is listed in every leaf its convex hull meets, tested by the cell's face planes against the 8 box corners, AABB test for non-convex cells) and `mset`. Splitting is xs-independent: split while distinct materials among the candidates exceed 1 and candidates exceed `leaf_cells`, else stop; candidate planes are 8 equally spaced per axis, cost = sum over children of `volume_fraction * (candidates + material_spread)`, ties by lowest axis then lowest position. A leaf whose box lies inside one convex cell (all 8 corners inside all planes) is `preassigned` (no candidate tests). A leaf with one material and one region stores `uniform_material`/`uniform_region` so a flight with `need_cell = false` never locates.
     - Build is rayon-parallel only in per-cell derivation and per-subtree recursion; output is collected in index order.
  3. **M2 flights** (`flight.rs`). `DeltaTracker::flight` per Contract. Carry the residual majorant optical depth `tau ~ Exp(1)` across segments (memoryless, so resampling per segment is equivalent and unnecessary). One uniform per tentative collision, plus one for the acceptance test iff `sigma_t < sigma_maj`. In a Surface segment `sigma_t == sigma_maj`, so no acceptance draw. Hinted location at a tentative collision: (a) `preassigned` leaf, no test; (b) the cell located at the previous tentative collision of this flight if the cached `TrackState::safety` (lower bound minus the distance flown) is positive, no test; (c) the leaf candidate list, ascending cell id, strictly-inside wins, boundary ties by the same rank rule as `locate` (lowest id whose direction enters, then tangent). Result must equal `locate(p, Some(dir), None)` for every point. `bvh_descents` stays 0 inside the partition; the cell BVH is only for points outside the box, `COMPLEMENT` and the first location of a flight with no hint. Traversal state lives in `Scratch` (hits/ids/work); zero heap allocation after warm-up. `sigma_t` is cached per material for the flight (energy is constant). A tentative collision with `sigma_t > sigma_maj` is `Err(RtError::MajorantViolation{..})` carrying node, material, band, `sigma_t`, `sigma_maj`, position, cell. It is never clamped, and no state is committed past the violating point.
     - Deck faces behave exactly as in `step` (Reflect and Wrap applied in flight; Respond leaves the state on the face; no RNG for decks). `FlightOptions{ stop_at_region: bool = false, need_cell: bool = true, max_segments }`: with `stop_at_region` the flight ends at every `RegionBoundary` and descends to Surface segments in leaves holding region faces, which is how surface-current tallies work.
  4. **M3 policy, safety, region jump.**
     - *Hybrid policy.* Build-time flags per (node, band) from `CostModel{c_plane, c_node, c_locate_candidate, c_rng}` (fixed documented work-unit constants, not measured time): `delta_ok = (1 - sigma_bar/M) <= tau` with `sigma_bar` the volume-weighted mean over the leaf's cells (`tau = 0.9` default), `delta_wins = C_DT < C_ST` with `n_b` from the mean chord `4V/A`. A leaf descends to Surface segments unless both flags hold. Run-time refinement (Serpent rule) at flight start on the current material: `sigma_t(current)/sigma_M < 1 - tau` forces Surface for this flight. `ModePolicy{ tau, block_materials, force_materials }` overrides (`blockdt`/`forcedt`). `TrackingMode::{Surface, Delta, Hybrid}` in `SegmentOptions`/`FlightOptions`.
     - *Safety.* `MeshTracker::safety(st)` is the exact distance from `st.pos` to the planes of the current convex cell (min over faces of `d - n.x`; 0.0 for a non-convex or facet cell, a valid lower bound). `TrackState::safety` caches a lower bound (decremented by the distance flown, reset to 0 on cell change). A flight shorter than the cached safety skips the exit test and the location. Outputs, RNG consumption and states are identical with the cache on or off (`HierarchyOptions::safety_cache`, default true).
     - *Region jump.* `HierarchyOptions::region_jump` (default false). When true, `MeshTracker::fire` is overridden: ordered traversal of a per-region BVH over the region's interface/boundary faces (built from the existing `face_bvh` face list, one BVH per region, deterministic median split), same exact fan-triangle predicate and SoS ties as `walk`, then `to_cell` from face adjacency and, for `Limit`, one hinted locate. Same end kind, surface, face, `to_region`, `to_cell` as the walk-based `fire`; distance within `1e-12 (1 + d)`. `step` and `trace` never jump (Sn/MOC and mesh tallies keep the cell walk).
  5. **M4 World** (`world.rs`, `WorldTracker`). `MeshTrackerBuilder` accepts `BuildInput::WorldOf(WorldInput{spec, leaves})`; leaves are `Mesh`/`Facets` inputs built by this crate into prototype trackers, each with its own kd partition and tables (shared by every instance). `Lattice` components (rect only) become lattice nodes with Amanatides-Woo DDA across elements, O(1) element index, element transform = translation; `Union` placements (rigid transforms: rotation matrix orthonormal within `1e-12`, else `world.nonrigid`) become universe nodes. Placements whose boxes overlap are `RtError::Build` `world.overlap` naming both (disjoint partition is the rule). Hex lattice is `Unsupported("hex lattice")`. Instances are implicit: global `CellId` = prefix sum over instances in ascending `InstanceId` (row-major, x fastest); a uniform-fill lattice computes `id * ncells_proto` with no per-instance memory; otherwise one `u32` prefix per element. Crossing an element wall ends a `step` with `SlotChange` and updates `levels`; a delta flight crosses it inside a segment stream with no event. `node_mset(lattice)` is the union of its fills; a lattice segment whose element is a single prototype leaf uses that leaf's majorant. Location at a tentative collision starts at the owning lattice element (index arithmetic), then the prototype leaf's candidate list. `caps() = HIERARCHY | DELTA` (plus `VOLUME_MESH | EXACT_PLANAR` from leaves).
  6. **Cache.** Hierarchy options are in `BuildOptions::hierarchy`; `cache_key().options` hashes the partition parameters and the `MaterialMap` but not xs values. `export_cache` writes the partition and candidate lists as `Private(4..8)` plus the `Hierarchy` tag for components; `load` recomputes msets, tables and band flags from the provided xs in O(msets x bands). A changed xs gives fresh tables from the same image; a changed `MaterialMap` or partition option gives `CacheStale`. `BUILDER_VERSION` stays 1; the bytes of hierarchy-less images are unchanged (golden hash asserted).
  7. **Plugin.** `RtPlugin` gains the read-only query `rt.partition {mesh: Mesh, leaf_cells?: u32 = 8, max_depth?: u32 = 24}` returning `{nodes, leaves, depth, preassigned_leaves, cells_per_leaf: {min, max, mean}, memory_bytes, structure_hash}` (structure only: the engine has no cross sections). `rt.fire` stays out.
  8. **Docs and bench.** `DESIGN.md` gets sections "Hierarchy", "Flights and estimators", "Policy and cost model", "Safety and region jump", "World"; `examples/bench_delta.rs` records, per mode (Surface, Delta, Hybrid) and per case, the work counters per track (segments, node_visits, plane_tests, locate_candidates, tentative, rejected, safety_skips, jumps) and ns/track at 1 and 4 threads with the CPU model: C-A 17x17 pin lattice (World of `hex_block(3)` pins, 3 materials), C-B large homogeneous region (`hex_block(40)`, walk vs `region_jump`), C-C two-material slab with a 20:1 contrast. `examples/trace_rays.rs` gains `--region-jump` so the sm-rt-capi harness can run the same facet rays both ways. No claim against DAGMC; numbers are copied into DESIGN.md with date and CPU.

- **Acceptance (analytic; fixed seeds from case names; debug runs use the stated sizes, the full runs are `#[ignore]` and run in release by the gate; statistical tests use 4 sigma on a fixed seed so they are deterministic, never flaky):**
  1. **Exact segment sums.** 1e5 seeded lines on `hex_block(10)`, `mixed_transition`, a 2-level lattice world: in every mode the sum of `t1 - t0` over segments equals the analytic hull chord within 1e-12; segments are contiguous (`t1[k] == t0[k+1]` bit-exact); every segment endpoint lies in its node box within `tol`; Surface segments equal `trace` segments bit for bit; 0 `Lost`.
  2. **Majorant never violated.** For every leaf and band, `M[mset][b] >= band_max(m, b)` for all m among cells overlapping the leaf (independent brute force in the test, using cell AABBs and exact plane tests); on 1e6 random points and 1e6 flight tentative collisions `sigma_t <= sigma_maj`; round-up bound `M <= band_max (1 + 2^-23)` for the maximizing material; `TableXs` with a sub-band resonance peak (piecewise-linear, peak between band edges) still gives a sound `M`.
  3. **Violation is an error.** `TableXs::with_understated_bound(0.5)` gives `Err(MajorantViolation)` with the exact node, material, band, `sigma_t`, `sigma_maj`, position and cell; the state is not advanced past the point; no panic; `code()` is the next free negative code.
  4. **Hinted location equals `locate`.** 1e6 seeded points (including 1e5 within 1e-9 of faces, edges, vertices) on `hex_block(10)` and `mixed_transition`: the flight's hinted cell equals `locate(p, Some(dir), None)`; counters show `bvh_descents == 0` for all flights starting inside the box; max candidate tests per location is at most `leaf_cells` x leaves met; preassigned leaves report 0 tests.
  5. **Unbiasedness, homogeneous slab.** `hex_block(20)` with mirror lateral decks (an infinite slab, thickness L = 20) and vacuum ends, pure absorber Sigma with tau = Sigma L in {0.1, 1, 10}, uniform isotropic volume source, 2e5 histories: leakage fraction equals `(1/tau)(1/2 - E3(tau))` (E3 by adaptive quadrature in the test to 1e-12) within 4 sigma (binomial); total flux `(1 - P_esc)/Sigma` from the track-length estimator (Surface) and the collision estimator (Delta) each within 4 sigma; repeated with `majorant_scale` in {1, 3, 10} (debug knob in `HierarchyOptions`, loosens the bound): estimates agree while `rejected / tentative` matches `1 - 1/scale` within 4 sigma (acceptance rate equals `sigma_bar/Sigma_M`).
  6. **Unbiasedness, two-region beam.** Slab with Sigma1 on x < a and Sigma2 beyond, beam along +x: transmission `exp(-(Sigma1 a + Sigma2 (L - a)))` within 4 sigma in Surface, Delta and Hybrid, with the kd partition separating the regions (assert the low-Sigma leaf has `M == Sigma_low`, so the vol-weighted tightness is at least 0.9 where the global majorant would give 0.5).
  7. **Estimator agreement, heterogeneous.** `hex_block(8)`, 4 materials by `(i + j + k) mod 4`, mirror on all sides, isotropic scattering with per-material scattering ratio c in {0.2, 0.5, 0.7, 0.9}, 2e5 histories: (i) balance identity `sum_c Sigma_a,c V_c phi_c = 1` for the track-length (Surface), collision (Delta) and mixed estimators within 4 sigma; (ii) per-cell flux from Surface/track-length vs Delta/collision vs Hybrid/mixed agree cell by cell within 5 sigma of the difference (sigma from 10 fixed history-id batches); (iii) a "checker hybrid" policy that forces Delta in even-numbered leaves and Surface in odd ones (test hook through `ModePolicy`) agrees with pure Surface on the same grid. Mixing rule under test: Surface segments score `w * length`, Delta segments score `w / sigma_t` at real collisions only.
  8. **Safety.** For 1e5 random interior points of convex cells, `safety(st)` is at most the fired distance along 1000 seeded directions, and the ray fired along `-n_min` has distance equal to `safety` within 1e-12; the cached `TrackState::safety` never exceeds the recomputed value (+1e-12) over 1e5-step histories; flights are identical with `safety_cache` on and off (same `FlightEnd`, positions bit for bit, RNG draws), and `safety_skips > 0` with strictly fewer `plane_tests` when on.
  9. **Region jump.** 1e6 seeded rays on `hex_block(20)` (single region) and on a two-region mesh with a non-convex region: `fire` with jump on vs off gives the same end kind, surface, face, `to_region`, `to_cell`, distance within `1e-12 (1 + d)`, and edge/vertex ties go to the lowest `FaceId` as in the walk; 0 `Lost`; on `hex_block(40)` the jump has strictly fewer `plane_tests + node_visits` per fire than the walk (ratio recorded in DESIGN.md).
  10. **Determinism.** `sm_testkit::determinism::check`: build bytes (partition, tables, flags) equal for `RAYON_NUM_THREADS=1` vs default and repeated; a 1e5-history batch (RNG = `HistoryRng::new(seed, history_id)`, tallies reduced over fixed blocks in block order) has equal tally bit-hash, counter totals and final-RNG hash in Surface, Delta, Hybrid and with region jump; `trace_bundle` content hash unchanged from W3a for every hierarchy-less fixture (golden values copied from the W3a tests).
  11. **Cache.** `build -> export_cache -> write_smrt -> read_smrt -> load(xs)` gives equal `structure_hash` and a bit-identical 1e5-history batch; loading with a different `TableXs` (same `MaterialMap`) succeeds and gives fresh tables; a different `MaterialMap` gives `CacheStale`; one flipped byte gives `CacheCorrupt`; no panic on a hostile image (candidate index out of range, cyclic kd nodes).
  12. **Errors and allocation.** Each new error path has a test (`MaterialMap` length, non-finite Sigma, `world.overlap`, `world.nonrigid`, hex lattice `Unsupported`, `MajorantViolation`, `BadDirection`); `flight`, `segments` and `fire` with a warmed `Scratch` allocate nothing at depth <= `LEVEL_INLINE` (counting allocator, extends `tests/alloc.rs`).
  13. **Lattice vs oracle.** A 4x4x1 lattice of `hex_block(3)` pins against `stub::AnalyticWorld::with_lattice` of the same shape: chord sums within 1e-12 for 1e5 lines, the same `SlotChange` sequence from `step`, and `fire` skips them; global `CellId`s follow the prefix-sum rule (checked at the first, last and a random interior element).
  14. **Lattice flights.** The unbiasedness tests 5 and 6 repeated on the lattice world (a 3x1x1 lattice of slab elements) in Delta and Hybrid agree with the single-mesh results within 4 sigma; at a tentative collision the owning element is found by index arithmetic (counter `lattice_index_locates` equals tentative count, `bvh_descents == 0`).
  15. **17x17 assembly counters (record, plus two gated invariants).** On C-A, `bvh_descents == 0` in Delta and Hybrid, and Hybrid's tentative-plus-segment count per track is at most min(Surface, Delta) + 5% (the policy never loses to the worse pure mode on its own cost counters); everything else is recorded only.
  16. Lints and docs: clippy `-D warnings`, rustdoc on every public item, `cargo build`, `cargo test -p sm-rt` with `RAYON_NUM_THREADS=1` and default.
